Questions to Ask Your LASIK Surgeon Before Committing

One of the most effective ways to distinguish the best LASIK surgeon in Mumbai from an average one is to ask pointed questions during your consultation and observe how they respond. An excellent surgeon will welcome these questions, answer them transparently, and never make you feel rushed. Here are the questions every patient should ask before proceeding with LASIK:

  1. Are you a fellowship-trained cornea and refractive specialist? This is the most fundamental question. A general ophthalmologist who also performs LASIK is not the same as a surgeon whose entire training and practice is focused on cornea and refractive surgery.
  2. How many LASIK procedures have you performed, and how many in the last year? Volume matters, but so does recent experience. A surgeon who did most of their LASIK a decade ago but rarely now is not ideal.
  3. What percentage of patients do you decline for LASIK? A good surgeon declines at least 10-20% of patients who are not suitable. If the answer is near zero, the surgeon may be prioritising revenue over safety.
  4. What alternative procedures do you offer if I am not a LASIK candidate? The best surgeons offer the full spectrum — including ICL implantation, surface ablation (PRK/SmartSurf), and refractive lens exchange — and recommend the right one for each patient.
  5. What is your enhancement or re-treatment rate? A low enhancement rate (under 2-3%) indicates precise surgical planning and execution. A high rate suggests less accurate nomograms or lax candidate selection.
  6. What is your post-operative follow-up schedule? You should hear about visits at one day, one week, one month, three months, six months, and one year — not a single check and discharge.
  7. What complications have you encountered, and how did you manage them? Any surgeon who claims they have never had a complication is either inexperienced or dishonest. The best surgeons are transparent about rare issues and how they were resolved.
