What Is ICL Surgery and Why Does It Cost What It Costs in Mumbai?

Implantable Collamer Lens surgery, commonly referred to as ICL, is an advanced refractive procedure designed for individuals whose vision correction needs fall outside the safe range of LASIK or other laser-based treatments. Instead of reshaping the cornea with a laser, ICL involves placing a permanently implanted lens inside the eye, behind the iris and in front of the natural crystalline lens. This lens works in much the same way as a contact lens — but because it sits inside the eye, there is no daily maintenance, no dryness, and no risk of losing or tearing a lens.

The reason ICL surgery cost in Mumbai is higher than standard LASIK pricing comes down to the technology involved. Each ICL is a precision-manufactured, biocompatible Collamer lens produced by STAAR Surgical — the only company in the world currently making these implantable lenses. These lenses are custom-ordered for each individual eye based on precise biometric measurements, and they are imported, which means the cost of the lens itself constitutes a significant portion of the total procedure price.

Beyond the lens, the cost reflects the expertise required to perform the procedure safely. ICL implantation is an intraocular surgery — meaning it takes place inside the eye, not just on its surface. This demands a surgeon with specialised training in both refractive and intraocular techniques, a fully equipped surgical theatre, and rigorous pre-operative assessment to ensure the eye's anatomy is suitable for the implant. When you pay for ICL surgery, you are paying for a combination of imported medical technology, surgical expertise, diagnostic precision, and post-operative care — all of which together determine the final price.

Who Needs ICL Surgery? Understanding Candidacy Beyond Cost

A thorough vision and anatomy assessment determines if ICL is your best option.

ICL surgery is specifically designed for patients who fall outside the safe and effective treatment range of LASIK and other laser-based refractive procedures. Understanding whether you fall into this category is the first step in determining whether ICL is a necessary investment for your vision.

The most common candidates for ICL surgery include individuals with high myopia (shortsightedness greater than -8.00 dioptres), where LASIK would require removing too much corneal tissue and compromise the structural integrity of the eye. Patients with thin corneas — typically below 480 microns — are also prime candidates, as LASIK further thins the cornea and can lead to complications such as ectasia. Those with moderate to severe dry eye syndrome often cannot tolerate LASIK, which exacerbates dryness by disrupting corneal nerves, whereas ICL leaves the cornea untouched.

Patients with high astigmatism that exceeds the corrective range of laser treatments, or those whose corneal topography shows early signs of keratoconus or forme fruste keratoconus, are also better served by ICL. Additionally, individuals who lead active lifestyles and are concerned about the long-term effects of corneal reshaping often prefer ICL because it preserves the natural corneal architecture entirely. The lens sits inside the eye without altering its external structure, making it an excellent choice for contact sport players, military personnel, and athletes.

ICL Lens Types — Visian ICL and EVO ICL Explained

When researching ICL surgery cost in Mumbai, you will encounter two primary lens models: the traditional Visian ICL and the newer EVO ICL. Understanding the difference between them helps explain some of the cost variation you may see across clinics.

Visian ICL (Traditional Model)

The original Visian ICL has been used worldwide for over two decades with an excellent safety record. This model requires a small peripheral iridotomy — a laser-created opening in the iris — before surgery to ensure proper fluid circulation within the eye and prevent pressure build-up. The iridotomy is performed as a separate quick laser procedure, usually a week or two before the ICL implantation. This adds a small additional step and cost to the overall treatment, but the lens itself is slightly less expensive than the EVO model.

EVO ICL (Latest Generation)

The EVO ICL features an integrated central port — a tiny opening in the centre of the lens that allows natural fluid flow within the eye, eliminating the need for a pre-operative iridotomy. This streamlines the procedure into a single surgical session and reduces overall treatment time. The EVO ICL also represents the latest evolution in Collamer lens design, with optimised geometry for improved visual outcomes. Because it is the newer technology with enhanced features, the EVO ICL lens costs slightly more than the traditional Visian ICL, but many patients find the convenience of a single procedure worth the additional investment.

Both lens models are FDA-approved, CE-marked, and have extensive clinical data supporting their safety and efficacy. During your consultation at Sentra Clinic, Dr. Shraddha Surekha will recommend the most appropriate lens model based on your eye anatomy, lifestyle, and budget — not on which option is more profitable for the clinic.

The ICL Surgery Procedure — What Happens on the Day of Treatment

Understanding the surgical process helps demystify the cost and reassures you about what to expect. ICL surgery is a precise, microsurgical procedure that typically takes 20-30 minutes per eye. Here is a step-by-step walkthrough of your surgical day at Sentra Clinic:

  1. Arrival and preparation: You arrive at our Malad East clinic about an hour before your scheduled surgery. Our team reviews your pre-operative measurements, administers dilating and anaesthetic eye drops, and ensures you are comfortable and relaxed.
  2. Final measurements and lens verification: Dr. Surekha performs a final slit-lamp examination and confirms the lens specifications match your eye's anatomy precisely. The custom-ordered ICL is unpacked and inspected under the microscope.
  3. Surgical preparation: You are taken to our surgical theatre. The area around your eye is sterilised, a sterile drape is placed, and a speculum gently holds your eyelids open. You remain awake throughout but feel no pain — only mild pressure.
  4. Lens implantation: Through a micro-incision of approximately 3 millimetres at the edge of the cornea, Dr. Surekha carefully inserts the folded ICL, which unfolds slowly into position behind your iris and in front of your natural lens. No stitches are required — the incision is self-sealing.
  5. Positioning and verification: The surgeon confirms the lens is correctly positioned and centred using the surgical microscope. For Toric ICLs, precise alignment to your astigmatism axis is verified. Intraocular pressure is checked to ensure normal fluid circulation.
  6. Recovery and same-day discharge: After a short observation period, you are examined once more and discharged the same day. Most patients notice significantly clearer vision within hours, though full stabilisation takes a few weeks. You will be prescribed antibiotic and anti-inflammatory eye drops and given detailed aftercare instructions.

ICL Surgery Recovery — Timeline and What to Expect

One of the advantages of ICL surgery is its relatively quick and comfortable recovery. Unlike LASIK, which can cause significant dry eye for weeks or months, ICL patients typically experience minimal discomfort because the cornea is not reshaped. Here is what the recovery timeline generally looks like:

🕐

First 24-48 Hours

You may experience mild light sensitivity, slight watering, or a foreign-body sensation. Vision is typically already much clearer than before surgery. You should rest, avoid rubbing your eyes, wear the protective shield at night, and use prescribed drops diligently.

📅

First Week

Most patients return to work and normal activities within 2-3 days. You will have a follow-up examination on day one and again at one week. Vision continues to sharpen as the eye heals. Avoid swimming, eye makeup, and strenuous exercise during this period.

🗓️

One Month and Beyond

By one month, your vision should be stable and clear. A comprehensive check-up confirms the lens position, intraocular pressure, and endothelial cell health. Long-term, you simply attend annual check-ups — the ICL is designed to remain in your eye for life without maintenance.
