Factors That Affect the Cost of LASIK Surgery

Understanding what drives the cost of LASIK surgery helps you make an informed decision and avoid clinics that cut corners on safety or technology. Here are the key factors that influence LASIK pricing:

🔬

Type of Technology Used

Blade-based LASIK uses a mechanical microkeratome and costs less. Blade-free Femto LASIK uses a femtosecond laser for greater precision and safety. Contoura Vision uses advanced topography-guided mapping for superior visual outcomes. The more advanced the technology, the higher the cost — but also the better the results.

👁️

Your Refractive Error

Higher prescriptions (very high myopia, astigmatism) may require more advanced procedures like Contoura Vision, SMILE, or ICL rather than standard LASIK. The complexity of your refractive error directly influences which procedure is safest and most effective for your eyes.

Pre- and Post-Operative Care

Why Choosing the Cheapest LASIK Can Be a Costly Mistake

You will see advertisements for LASIK at dramatically different price points — from unbelievably cheap to premium. It is natural to be drawn to the lowest price, but when it comes to your eyes, the cheapest option is rarely the safest. Here is why:

  • Older technology: Clinics offering very low prices often use older-generation laser systems that lack the precision, eye-tracking, and customisation capabilities of modern platforms. This can result in under-correction, over-correction, glare, halos, or the need for enhancement surgery.
  • Hidden charges: The advertised price may cover only the laser procedure. Pre-operative testing, post-operative medications, follow-up visits, and enhancement procedures are billed separately — sometimes doubling the final cost.
  • Inexperienced surgeons: Low-cost clinics may use junior surgeons or rotating visiting surgeons who perform fewer procedures per year. LASIK is a precision surgery where surgeon experience directly correlates with outcomes.
  • Skipped safety protocols: To reduce costs, some clinics skip essential pre-operative tests like pachymetry (corneal thickness), topography, or dry eye evaluation — increasing the risk of complications like corneal ectasia.
  • No enhancement warranty: If your vision regresses and you need an enhancement procedure, a clinic that did not include this in the original package will charge you again — sometimes at full price.

At Sentra Clinic, we do not compete on price — we compete on quality, safety, and outcomes. Our pricing is fair and transparent, reflecting the advanced technology, experienced surgeons, and comprehensive care you receive. When it comes to your eyes, value matters more than cost.
