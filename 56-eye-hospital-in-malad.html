What to Expect When You Visit Our Eye Hospital in Malad

We understand that visiting a hospital can feel intimidating, especially when it concerns your vision. Here is exactly what happens when you come to Sentra Clinic for the first time:

  1. Booking your appointment: Call us at 93729 47075 or message us on WhatsApp. Our team will schedule you at a convenient time and let you know if any preparation is needed.
  2. Registration and history: On arrival, our team records your basic details, medical history, current medications, and any eye complaints. This takes about 5-10 minutes.
  3. Initial testing: A trained technician performs preliminary tests — visual acuity, refraction, intraocular pressure, and slit-lamp examination — which the surgeon reviews before seeing you.
  4. Surgeon consultation: Dr. Modi or Dr. Surekha personally examines your eyes, reviews the preliminary findings, explains what they see, and recommends any additional tests or treatment.
  5. Advanced diagnostics (if needed): If your condition requires further investigation — OCT, fundus photography, topography, perimetry — these are performed on-site during the same visit.
  6. Treatment plan and discussion: The surgeon explains your diagnosis, discusses all treatment options, answers your questions in your preferred language, and provides a clear cost estimate before you commit to anything.
  7. Scheduling (if surgery is needed): If surgery is recommended, we discuss dates, pre-operative preparation, insurance coverage, and recovery expectations — all before you leave.
Tip: If you are coming for a comprehensive eye checkup, expect the visit to take 60-90 minutes. Dilating drops may be used, which can blur near vision for 2-3 hours — so we recommend not driving yourself home. Bring sunglasses for comfort.
